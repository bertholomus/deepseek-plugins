## Findings (most severe first: blocker, major, minor, nit; each with where, what, and the fix)

### Minor

1. **A carry-on turn that fails is never tried again when the session has no todos and no goal, and its row says only *failed*.**
   - Where: `plugins/autopilot/lib/pilot.js`. `onTurnEnd()` line 464 cancels a failed turn without open work (`else if (!hasOpenWork(state)) this.cancel(sessionId)`). `hasOpenWork()` (line 74) counts only open todos and an active goal. The start and switch scan (line 599) skips such a session too. `holdOf()` (line 1032) returns no hold, so the row gets no why.
   - What:
     - The lighthouse story's carry-on turn 3 (sent 3:11:07 after the second restart) failed with "Connection error." at 3:13:10 when the gateway went away; the color question's carry-on turn 4 (sent 3:26:13 after the settings restart) failed at 3:26:28. Both are in the sessions' own logs as `turn/end` with `kind: error`, and no turn came after.
     - At 3:44 PM both had sat *failed* for 33 and 18 minutes: "last: told it to carry on after DSH stopped", no why, no next step, nothing in "Waiting for you", no Try again (pictures 11, 12, 22, 24, 26, 31). Meanwhile bird names, with todos, got three note-first tries and then *gave up* with Try again.
     - This is the power-blip case in the brief's list of workarounds: DSH comes back before the gateway and the lanes, so every carry-on note fails once. Sessions with a todo list or a goal are retried; any other cut-off session stops for good, and its row looks as if it was handled.
   - Fix: count "the turn Autopilot's own carry-on note started failed" as open work (the cut-off work is still open). Send it down the error path: note-first tries on `errorRetrySeconds`, then *gave up* with Try again in "Waiting for you". At the least, give a failed row a why and Try again ("its carry-on turn failed (Connection error.); Autopilot retries only sessions with todos or a goal"). Test it: a unit case on the fake DSH (cut off, carry-on, error) and an e2e step (an outage right after a restart).

2. **An offer anywhere in a reply's last six lines holds the idle nudge, and the row says the reply asks you something.**
   - Where: `shared/host/words.js` `OFFERS` (line 226: "let me know", "if you('d| would) (like|want|prefer)", "happy to", "feel free"), used by `asksSomeone()` (line 252), which `pilot.js` uses for the idle check (line 658) and the row's hold (line 1042).
   - What:
     - I ran `asksSomeone()` over nine endings. These four are held, though none asks anything and three say the agent will go on: "All tests pass. Happy to keep going with the next todo.", "I am happy to report the build is green. Moving on to the docs next.", "Feel free to review the diff; I will carry on with the remaining two todos.", "If you would like a different name, say so. Next up: the README." A plain question, `DECISION NEEDED` and "Let me know if you want me to continue with steps 2 and 3." are held too, as they should be.
     - With open todos such a session gets no nudge, and its row says "Waits for you: its last reply asks you something." Local models end many replies with lines like these, so the miss lands on the job the plugin exists for.
     - The README (lines 29 and 30) names a question, `DECISION NEEDED` and `BLOCKED:` as what holds it, not offers. The shared test (`test/suite-shared.test.js` line 136) asserts the hold for one offer; nothing tests the false holds.
   - Fix: hold only for offers that hand the next step to the person ("let me know if/when …", "if you'd like me to …"), and not when the reply's last line states the next step ("Next:", "I will carry on", "Moving on"). Say it in the README. Test it with a corpus of real endings from the stack's sessions and report the false-hold rate, as Proof does for its flags.
