## Findings (most severe first: blocker, major, minor, nit; each with where, what, and the fix)

1. **Major (for pictures of this kind): the veil is set from the whole picture, so a small dark or light part under the sidebar or the composer is missed.** Where: `lib/client.js`, `worstCell` (the 20th percentile in light, the 80th in dark, of the picture drawn at 24 × 16) and `veilFor`. What: in light mode a picture whose dark part is under a fifth of the frame gets little or no veil, and where that part sits under DSH's text the text falls well under the plugin's own bar: the split picture (a dark strip under the sidebar, veil .35): "Workspaces" 2.16:1 (DSH alone 3.55), the times 1.93:1 (3.19); the dark-foreground picture (veil 0): the status line under the composer 2.45:1 (3.58). Landscapes with a dark tree line at one edge or a dark shore along the bottom are this kind of picture, and the README's heading promises "DSH's words stay readable over any picture". Fix: sample the cells under each surface that carries text (the sidebar, the chat card, the composer and its status line, the dock, the new-chat words), mapped through the wall's cover crop, and take the strongest veil those regions need; recompute on resize and when the sidebar's width changes; add a split and a dark-foreground picture to the e2e's pixel step in both modes; say in the README what the veil samples.
2. **Minor: Wallpaper's own tile names fall under 4.5:1 over such pictures.** Where: `lib/client.js`, the tiles' colour `color-mix(in srgb, var(--dsw-alias-label-secondary) 80%, var(--dsw-alias-label-primary))`. What: "Plugins", "Automation tasks" and "More" at 4.12:1 in light over the split picture and "More" at 4.35:1 in dark over the dark-foreground picture, where DSH's own list shows them in its primary label colour (18:1 and 10.6:1). Fix: use `--dsw-alias-label-primary` for tile names, as DSH's list does, or check the mix at 4.5:1 over the worst ground in the e2e.
3. **Minor (the owner's decision): light mode veils most photos to about a quarter of their strength.** Where: `lib/client.js` `veilFor`; README Limits; DESIGN.md's open question on deepened labels. What: at the default 55% tint the light veil was .81 on the night photo, .76 on the clouds and .74 on the coast, close to DESIGN.md's mid-tone .71. The README's Limits give a dark picture .81 in light and a light one .51 in dark, and "a picture that fits the mode, none"; but the cloud photo, light enough to get no veil in dark, got .76 in light, so an owner won't expect a washed-out wall in daylight for ordinary photos. Fix: state the mid-tone numbers in Limits; bring the owner the deepened-labels question with them (DESIGN.md's own estimate: mid-tone light .71 to .21); a regional veil (finding 1) would also leave the bare wall stronger where no text sits.
4. **Minor: the tests can't see finding 1.** Where: `test/e2e/run.mjs` readability step (even gradient pictures) and `test/unit.test.mjs` (the veil test restates the 20th-percentile rule). What: every e2e picture is uniform enough that any percentile gives the same answer, and the unit test checks the rule, not the outcome. Fix: e2e pictures with a dark strip under the sidebar and a dark foreground under the composer, measured in both modes; a unit test that asserts the contrast under each surface for such pictures.
5. **Nit: one e2e picture shows no dialog.** Where: `test/e2e/run.mjs` lines 623 to 645, `panel-dark.png` on 0.2.0-rc.2. What: the picture shows the open chat without the panel; the check reads computed colours from the DOM, so it passed, but the evidence doesn't show it. Fix: wait for the dialog to be visible (its box and opacity) before the shot.
6. **Nit: the README's e2e time is stale.** Where: README Tests ("1 to 2 minutes each"). What: 440 to 565 s a build here (the machine was shared). Fix: give the measured times.
7. **Nit: another open window doesn't follow a change.** Where: `lib/client.js` (no read of `/api/wallpaper/state` after start; the 60 s tick re-applies local state only). What: a setting changed in one window reaches another open window only on a reload or when its panel opens. Fix: re-read the state on the 60 s tick or on window focus, or say so in the README.
8. **Nit (read in the code, not timed): thumbnails load the full 2880 px picture.** Where: `lib/client.js`, the thumbnails' `src: urlOf(f)` (with `loading: "lazy"`). What: each 90 px tile decodes a 2880 px image, and the first view of a new collection runs sips once per large photo. Fix: a small cached size for thumbnails (for example `&w=320` in `wp-cache`).
9. **Nit: a first save logs `wallpaperKind undefined -> "image"`.** Where: `lib/impl.js` `changePrefs`. Fix: write "(not set)" for a missing value.
10. **Nit (read in the code, not run): a rename's settings write is outside any try.** Where: `lib/impl.js` line 309 (`collectionRename`) and its route at line 355. What: if the chosen collection is renamed and the settings write then fails (a read-only data folder beside a writable library, the e2e's own failure case), the throw leaves the route unhandled, with no worded error and no log line, after the folder was renamed; the saved choice still names the old folder. It is the one uncaught path I found. Fix: catch it, log it and answer 500 "Renamed, but couldn't save the choice", as `changePrefs` does at line 328.
