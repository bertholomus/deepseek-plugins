## Findings (most severe first: blocker, major, minor, nit; each with where, what, and the fix)

1. **Minor, but silent: a note's sections past the 400th are left out without a word, and "Only its first 4 MB are searched" overstates what is read.** Where: `lib/handoffs.js` (`NOTE_LIMITS.sections: 400`; `readNote` stops at 400 rows, and the 8,000-character parts of a long section count as rows); `lib/indexer.js`, `readHandoff` (it marks a note read in part only when the file is over 4 MB); the README (the notes row of what it reads, the Index card, the limits: only the 4 MB rule); `test/recall-host.test.js` (the 4 MB test, about line 1126). What: my `daily-log.md` (26 KB, one heading a day, Day 0 to Day 600): "Entry 450" and "walrus ferret" (Day 600) give "Nothing matches"; its folder says 805 sections and *up to date*; the note is not under Files read in part; the newest turns show its newest section as "Day 398" (pictures `index-states3`, `folder-menu`). My `big-note.md` (4.7 MB) is listed as "Only its first 4 MB are searched", but those 4 MB hold 38,685 sections and Recall keeps 400, the first 42 KB (its 401st section starts at byte 41,725): "maintenance number 399", in its 401st section, gives "Nothing matches". A note without headings keeps 400 parts of at most 8,000 characters, at most about 3.2 MB of the 4 MB promised; the unit test of the 4 MB rule writes such a note but searches only a word at the start and one past 4 MB, so it passes. A daily or running log in a notes folder loses its newest entries this way once it passes 400 headings, while the page says all is up to date. Fix: make the 4 MB promise true (cap a note by bytes, or raise the row cap so 4 MB fits), or else mark a note cut by the row cap as read in part ("Only its first 400 sections are searched.") and, for a log that grows at the bottom, keep its newest sections; name the cap in the README; add tests: a 401-section note marked and its 401st section found or the cut said, and a word just under 4 MB found.
2. **Minor: the reading view shows a reply's Markdown as raw text.** Where: `lib/client.js`, the reading view (about lines 2066 to 2074): the reply (and a note section) go into a `pre-wrap` block as they are. What: the heron session's turn read in Recall shows `**"Ocelot ingest handoff"**`, backticks, `[data/notes/HANDOFF.md](~/…)` and `## Next steps` (pictures `reading-session`), while DSH's chat draws the same reply with bold, code and a link, and Recall's own snippets strip the marks. Fix: draw replies with DSH's own Markdown component, as the chat does, or strip the marks as the snippets do, keeping code blocks.
