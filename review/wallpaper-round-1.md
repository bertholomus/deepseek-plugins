## Findings (most severe first: blocker, major, minor, nit; each with where, what, and the fix)

1. **Major: DSH's own text is not readable at the defaults over a picture of the other tone.** Where: `lib/client.js` style sheet (the wall's scrim, the glass rules, the hero at line 574; the headline's halo at 632–633 covers only Wallpaper's own headline), README Limits. What: at the default 55% tint, light mode over a dark picture: the sidebar's labels and times 1.6:1, the composer's placeholder and "High" 1.0:1 (gone), the new-chat words 2.4:1; dark mode over a light picture: the new-chat words 2.0:1 and the chips 2.2:1 on the bare wall, the sidebar's labels 3.3:1, the open chat's grey labels 3.3:1. Light on light and dark on dark read fine, but a rotating collection in System mode lands in the bad cases every day, and the job asks for "readable in light and dark". Fix: (a) on Wallpaper's own wall: when a picture loads, take its mean luminance (draw it to a 16×16 canvas; the route is same-origin) and deepen the scrim toward the mode's ground when the picture is far from it; (b) a ground under the new-chat words: a soft radial scrim on the wall behind `_composerHero`, or the halo of lines 632–633 also when no headline is set; (c) bring the owner DESIGN.md's two open questions with these numbers (deepened labels through `ctx.theme.overrideTokens`; a floor for dense text such as `max(.75, tint + .2)`); (d) an e2e check that measures DSH's own text (sidebar, composer, new-chat words, open chat) from pixels over a light and a dark picture in both modes at 55%; (e) README Limits: say it happens at the default tint, in both mismatched cases, and on the new-chat page.
2. **Major: an opaque band behind the composer in every open chat.** Where: `lib/client.js`, the composer rules (lines 625–628); DSH's `[data-slot="main.conversation"] [class*="_composerSeat"]`. What: DSH's composer seat paints `linear-gradient(transparent 0, #fff 36px)` (the dark ground in dark), 130 px tall, between the glass composer and the glass chat card, so a white strip crosses the bottom of the chat over a darker picture (`chat-over-dark-picture-light.png`, `after-restart-interrupted-light.png`). The e2e's composer check reads only the card's own background, so it passes. Fix: `body.wp-on [data-slot="main.conversation"] [class*="_composerSeat"]{background:none!important}` (anchored like the `_fade` rule at line 612), and an e2e check that walks from the composer card up to the chat card and fails on any painted ground, `background-image` included.
3. **Minor: a non-picture file with a picture's extension is accepted and freezes the wall.** Where: `lib/impl.js` `wallpaperAdd` (lines 216–226: the extension and the data-URL prefix are the only checks) and `wallpaperFile` (206–207); `lib/client.js` rotation (line 80) and `failedKey` (505, 520). What: a 49-byte text file named `.jpg` was "Added"; its tile is a broken image; when rotation lands on it the wall keeps the previous picture for the whole period while the panel marks the broken tile as on the wall; Rotate off then keeps the broken file; `sips` runs again on every request, with a multi-line error in `wallpaper.log` each time. Fix: check the first bytes on add (JPEG `FF D8 FF`, PNG `89 50 4E 47`, WebP `RIFF…WEBP`, HEIC `ftyp` heic, heix or mif1) and refuse in words; in the window, move on to the next picture when one fails to load and mark its tile "can't be shown"; remember a failed resize per file and modified time so the log gets one line.
4. **Minor: un-keeping after Rotate off doesn't go back to rotating.** Where: `lib/client.js` line 326. What: Rotate switched off keeps the shown picture; clicking it again clears the kept picture but leaves `wallpaperRotate: "off"` (saved: kept none, rotate off, the switch off), while the panel and the README say "click it again to go back to rotating". Fix: send `wallpaperRotate: null` with `wallpaperPinned: null` when un-keeping, and test it.
5. **Minor: a "Couldn't save" note outlives the problem.** Where: `lib/client.js` `flush` (lines 131–136). What: after a refused write, a later change saved fine and the red "Couldn't save: couldn't write the settings file (no permission)." stayed (`trash-question-*.png`). Fix: on a successful save, clear that section's error note.
6. **Nit: collection names.** Where: `lib/impl.js` line 37 (`SAFE_NAME`), 256, 266. What: a case-only rename ("Judge" to "judge") is refused with "judge already exists" (the disk ignores case); names with letters outside ASCII (é, ü, Japanese) are refused, and such folders made in Finder are left out of the panel without a word. Fix: allow Unicode letters and digits (`\p{L}\p{N}` with the `u` flag) and still refuse separators and a leading dot; do a case-only rename through a temporary name; say in the panel which folders were skipped and why.
7. **Nit: a long headline runs off the window.** Where: `lib/client.js` line 632. What: 120 characters without a space overflow to the right (`headline-long-light.png`). Fix: `overflow-wrap:anywhere` on the headline.
8. **Nit: a write failure answers 400.** Where: `lib/impl.js` line 298. What: the window's console logs "400 (Bad Request)" for a fault of the disk. Fix: 500 for write failures, 400 only for refused values.
9. **Nit: removing the colour on the wall leaves it there.** Where: `lib/client.js` line 346. Fix: when it is the shown colour, switch to the first preset, or say that it stays until another is picked.
10. **Nit (read in the code, not run): picking a colour stops picture rotation.** Where: `lib/client.js` line 297 saves `wallpaperRotate: "off"`, and choosing a collection (line 320) does not reset it, so pictures stay still after a colour was tried. The switch shows it, so nothing is hidden. Fix: rotate again when a collection is chosen, or keep one rotate setting per kind.
11. **Nit: config problems show only in the log.** What: bad keys are logged in words, as the README says, but nothing in the window tells the owner, and `dsh-test doctor` printed the row's keys (the unknown `colour` included) without a warning, while the README says doctor lists none. Fix: one line at the top of the panel when the row had problems, pointing at `wallpaper.log`; README: doctor shows the row's keys but does not check them.
